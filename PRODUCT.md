# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Egyptian property owners and buyers in the process of receiving (handover/تسليم) or finishing (تشطيب) residential or commercial units (apartments, duplexes, villas, offices) across Greater Cairo and Egypt, whether in compounds, urban developments, or standalone residential buildings. They face high anxiety over contractor fraud, hidden defects, substandard MEP installations, and expensive rework.

## Product Purpose

Itqan (إتقان) provides independent, certified engineering quality control, inspection, and handover supervision. Payments and commercial contracts are managed directly between the client and the finishing office; Itqan acts strictly as the independent technical authority providing certified diagnostic reports and punch-lists based on the Egyptian Building Code. This gives the client full technical leverage to inspect and verify every milestone before settling their contractor dues.

## Positioning

Independent engineering authority and empirical measurement vs. uncertified contractor promises. Unlike finishing companies that supervise their own work or non-technical inspectors, Itqan is 100% independent and inspects using calibrated scientific instruments (Bosch 360° laser levels, 15-bar hydraulic piping test pumps, digital cable/copper scanners, moisture detectors) strictly conforming to Egyptian Building Codes (الكود المصري للبناء).

## Operating Context

- **Environment:** High-stakes handover meetings between homeowners, developers, and finishing contractors on-site.
- **Digital Traffic:** Over 80% mobile traffic originating from targeted social ad campaigns (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok).
- **Core Rituals:** Requesting an inspection session, receiving on-site diagnostic checks, reviewing the official engineering punch-list/report, and presenting the report to developers/contractors for rectification before signing delivery certificates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Language & Orientation:** Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) / Egyptian professional business Arabic, strictly right-to-left (`dir="rtl"`).
- **Form & Conversion:** High-converting single-page landing funnel with immediate Egyptian mobile phone number validation (`010`, `011`, `012`, `015`), unit type and location selection, and instant WhatsApp / booking dispatch.
- **Technical Stack:** Lightweight semantic HTML5, Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript, custom CSS with responsive mobile-first architecture. Ready for Firebase Firestore lead ingestion.

## Evidence on Hand

- Standard operating procedures for MEP & architectural inspection:
  - Bosch 360° laser leveling for floor, wall, and tile orthogonality/planarity.
  - 15-bar hydraulic pressure pump testing for concealed plumbing pipes.
  - Cable diameter, insulation resistance, and copper authenticity check (El Sewedy specifications).
  - Sound and hollow tile impact testing (مطرقة الفحص الهندسية).
- Egyptian Building Code (الكود المصري لأسس تصميم وشروط تنفيذ أعمال البناء والتشطيبات).

## Product Principles

1. **Uncompromised Independence:** Itqan never executes finishing contracts or takes kickbacks; our only loyalty is the property owner's structural and financial safety.
2. **Scientific Proof Over Opinion:** Every recorded defect is backed by calibrated instrument readings, photos, and Egyptian Code clauses.
3. **Frictionless Mobile Conversion:** Homeowners on mobile need clear answers, transparent scope, and single-click booking without complex multi-step barriers.
4. **Empowering the Homeowner:** Turn complex engineering jargon into clear, actionable punch-lists that force contractors and developers to fix defects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Proper semantic HTML5 hierarchy and screen-reader navigable RTL content.
- High color contrast between text, badges, and backgrounds to ensure readability outdoors under bright sunlight during site visits.
- Large, comfortable touch targets (minimum 44x44px) optimized for one-handed mobile use.
