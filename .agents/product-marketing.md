# Product Marketing Context: إتقان (Itqan)

## 1. Product Overview
- **Name:** إتقان (Itqan) | المنصة والرقابة الهندسية المستقلة
- **Category:** Independent Engineering Inspection & Quality Control for Finishing & Handover (استلام وتشطيب كافة الوحدات السكنية والتجارية)
- **Target Geography:** Egypt (Greater Cairo: All districts, whether in new developments, established residential neighborhoods, or standalone buildings).
- **Scope of Properties:** All residential and commercial units (شقق، دوبلكس، فيلات، مقرات إدارية وتجارية - سواء داخل كمبوند أو عمارات سكنية مستقلة).
- **Primary Conversion Goal:** Requesting an Engineering Inspection Session / Booking an inspection (حجز مهندس فحص معتمد) via Form & Direct WhatsApp.

## 2. Payment & Business Model Reality
- **Payment Handling:** Itqan has **NO control over financial transactions or milestone payments**. All financial arrangements, contracts, and milestone disbursements are handled **directly between the client and the chosen finishing office**.
- **Itqan's Value in Milestones:** Itqan acts strictly as the **independent technical authority**; we provide the client with certified on-site diagnostic reports and official punch-lists (محاضر استلام هندسية معتمدة) that give the client the technical evidence and leverage to evaluate each milestone accurately before settling payments with their contractor.

## 3. Target Audience & ICP
- **Who:** Property owners in Egypt finishing or receiving any unit (شقة، فيلا، دوبلكس، مكتب).
- **Key Anxieties & Pains:**
  - Fear of contractor fraud (غش المقاولين والتلاعب في الخامات).
  - Fear of catastrophic concealed defects (leaks in pipes behind tiles, non-original electrical wires, hollow tiles, sloping walls/floors).
  - Lack of technical engineering knowledge to challenge contractors during handover.
  - Financial stress and the cost of expensive rework after move-in (تكلفة التكسير بعد السكن).

## 4. Unique Value Proposition & Differentiators
- **100% Independent:** Itqan does not execute finishing work or receive kickbacks from contractors. Our only loyalty is the client's technical protection.
- **Empirical Diagnostics (أجهزة قياس معملية وهندسية):**
  - Bosch 360° laser levels (فحص استواء وميول الجدران والأرضيات).
  - 15-bar hydraulic piping pressure pumps (اختبار شبكة تغذية السباكة المدفونة ضد التسريب).
  - Digital scanners for copper authenticity & insulation resistance (كشف أصالة كابلات السويدي).
  - Sound resonance hammers (مطرقة الفحص لكشف الفراغات والتطبيل خلف السيراميك).
- **Compliance with the Egyptian Building Code (الكود المصري للبناء):** Reports cite exact engineering code tolerances and clauses.
- **Authoritative Punch-List Report (تقرير استلام هندسي رسمي):** A signed, stamped diagnostic report given to the client at each milestone.

## 5. Key Objections & Answers
- *"Do you handle my payments to the contractor?"* No, your financial relationship and payments are 100% directly between you and the contractor/office. We provide you with the unbiased technical inspection reports so you know exactly what has been done right and what needs fixing.
- *"Is your service only for compounds?"* No, our certified engineers inspect any residential apartment, villa, duplex, or commercial unit across Greater Cairo, whether in a compound, standalone building, or traditional neighborhood.
- *"Why can't I just inspect it myself?"* Concealed MEP defects (micro-leaks inside walls, counterfeit wire gauges) cannot be detected by eye until damage occurs months later.
- *"How fast is the report?"* Immediate preliminary summary on-site followed by a full digital report within 24 hours.
